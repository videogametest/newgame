# Stane's men

The defenders of the three places, the tax cart, and what the hero overhears. Use the names, natures, and lines exactly as written. `GAME_DESIGN.md` section 5 has the rules.

## How to read this

- **Type** is the unit type from section 5. Shield-bearers, brutes, and runners arrive with task 005; until then, treat a shield-bearer or brute as a soldier and a runner as a soldier who goes for the bell.
- **Loyalty** is how firmly they are Stane's: low, middling, or high. It sets how likely they are to stay in Wick if won back, and how soon they break. Map the three words to numbers in tuning.
- **Was** is the trade they had before. It is what they take up again if they stay, and it fills `{trade}` in Hesper's line.
- **Habit** is something they do every day that the player can use.
- They are people, not villains. They joined because Stane's men ate, or because they were soldiers and he was the only officer left. Most have not hurt anyone in years. Write any further lines for them in that spirit.
- Nobody here uses a pronoun for Wren, and nobody knows why the beacon was lit.

## Overheard lines

Heard only while the hero is close and unnoticed. Each exchange is said once per visit, at the time of day given, by the people named. If one of the speakers is gone, skip it.

Three kinds:

- **Information** the player can act on.
- **Story**, which adds to the picture without answering the question.
- **A tell**, which marks someone who would stay in Wick if spared.

## The Mill

The first place. Six defenders and their leader. It should teach watching before acting.

| Name | Type | Loyalty | Was | Habit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Harl Gadd** (leader) | Soldier | High | A carter's guard | Walks the whole yard at every guard change |
| Dob Finch | Soldier | Low | A miller's hand, at this mill | Feeds the geese at dawn. They are quiet while he does |
| Wyll Carrow | Archer | Middling | A fowler | Fishes alone at the mill pond at dusk |
| Bran Oakes | Soldier | Middling | A ploughman | Dozes on the night watch, against the woodpile |
| Nessa Roke | Archer | High | A guardsman's daughter | None. She is the one who is always looking |
| Pim | Runner | Low | An errand boy. The youngest here | Goes for the bell |
| Odo Varn | Soldier | Middling | A drover | Eats last, alone, after the others |

`overheard.mill.1` — information (midday, at the fire: Harl, Bran)

1. Harl: "Change at dusk and again at the middle of the night. If I find that woodpile warm again, Oakes, you're on the bell for a month."
2. Bran: "I rest my eyes. I don't sleep."

`overheard.mill.2` — information (morning, by the geese: Dob, Pim)

1. Pim: "They never honk at you."
2. Dob: "They know who feeds them. Anyone else comes within ten paces at a run, the whole valley hears about it."

`overheard.mill.3` — a tell (any time, at the wheel: Dob, Odo)

1. Dob: "I dressed these stones for old Marrin when I was twelve. Now I stand next to them with a spear."
2. Odo: "Keep that to yourself."

`overheard.mill.4` — story (dusk, at the fire: Nessa, Wyll)

1. Wyll: "You ever ask the Captain what was at the other end of that road?"
2. Nessa: "Once. He said he didn't know and it was the only order he ever disobeyed. Then he gave me double watch."

`overheard.mill.5` — information (night watch: Harl, Nessa)

1. Harl: "Strongbox key stays on my belt. Not on the hook, not under the sack. On my belt."

## The Forge

The second place. Seven defenders and their leader. Tobin Pike is here.

| Name | Type | Loyalty | Was | Habit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rafe Dunmore** (leader) | Brute | High | A drill sergeant's corporal | Works the anvil himself at midday, badly, with Aldric's hammer |
| Tobin Pike | Soldier | Low | A farmer's son, from Wick | Stands at the east fence at dusk, looking toward the village |
| Peg Rudge | Soldier | Low | A smith's striker, at this forge | Tends the fire before dawn. Alone with it for an hour |
| Hob Tarn | Brute | High | A quarryman | Sleeps across the strongroom door |
| Sib Marlowe | Shield-bearer | Middling | A cooper | Walks the same circuit, never varying it |
| Jory Flint | Archer | Middling | A fletcher's boy | Slips out to hunt rabbits most afternoons |
| Alda Grice | Archer | High | A guardsman | Watches the road from the roof |
| Ketch | Runner | Low | A shepherd's lad, who worked for the Sedges | Goes for the bell |

`overheard.forge.1` — a tell (dusk, at the east fence: Tobin, Ketch)

1. Ketch: "You're looking at it again."
2. Tobin: "There's four lights down there now. There was one when I came up here."
3. Tobin: "I never wanted this. I wanted supper. I was fifteen."

`overheard.forge.2` — information (midday, at the anvil: Rafe, Peg)

1. Peg: "You're holding it wrong. Aldric would have your ears."
2. Rafe: "Aldric's not here. His hammer is. That's the bit that matters."

`overheard.forge.3` — information (night: Sib, Alda)

1. Alda: "Tarn sleeps on the strongroom step. You'd have to move him to get in. You couldn't move him with an ox."
2. Sib: "There's a coal hatch round the back. Not that I've looked."

`overheard.forge.4` — story (at the fire, evening: Peg, Jory)

1. Jory: "Is it true the old guard all marched off in one night?"
2. Peg: "I was there. Striker's boy. We'd a cart half shod in the yard and they just left it. Never came back for the horse."

`overheard.forge.5` — a tell (before dawn, at the fire: Peg, alone)

1. Peg, to the fire: "There you are. Thirty years I've lit you. I'd light you for him again, if he'd have me."

`overheard.forge.6` — story (evening, in the yard: Rafe, Alda). So the turn is seen coming.

1. Alda: "The Captain's had word about the Mill."
2. Rafe: "And?"
3. Alda: "And he's not a man who leaves a thing where it fell."

## The Watch-house

Stane's outpost. Eight in his guard. A careful scout can thin them before the fight.

| Name | Type | Loyalty | Was | Habit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Captain Edric Stane** | See section 9 | | | Cannot be surprised |
| Lieutenant Corwin Vale | Shield-bearer | High | A guardsman. One of those who stayed | Inspects the gate at dusk and dawn |
| Marek Stroud | Brute | High | A guardsman | Never far from Stane |
| Ilsa Thorn | Archer | High | A huntsman | On the wall walk, always |
| Gant | Soldier | Middling | A guardsman | Leads the tax cart |
| Dunstan Lowe | Soldier | Middling | A steward's boy | Dusts the supper table every morning, on Stane's order, and moves nothing |
| Tom Sable | Shield-bearer | Middling | A wheelwright | Takes the midnight watch at the back gate |
| Rikke | Runner | Middling | A kennel hand | Rides with the tax cart |
| Hew Pollard | Archer | Low | A thatcher, from Wick | Rides with the tax cart, and hates it |

`overheard.watch.1` — story (morning, in the hall: Dunstan, Tom)

1. Tom: "Twenty years you've dusted round those bowls."
2. Dunstan: "He says they'll want their supper when they come back."
3. Tom: "He doesn't believe that."
4. Dunstan: "No. But he says it."

`overheard.watch.2` — information (dusk, at the gate: Vale, Tom)

1. Vale: "Back gate at midnight is yours. One man. I've asked for two and he says we've none to spare."

`overheard.watch.3` — story (night, on the wall: Ilsa, Marek)

1. Ilsa: "He stands up here and looks at that lamp in the village. Every night."
2. Marek: "He knew her. Before."
3. Ilsa: "He's never once gone down."

`overheard.watch.4` — a tell (any time: Hew, Rikke)

1. Hew: "I thatched half those roofs. Now I knock on the doors under them and ask for a tenth."
2. Rikke: "Then don't knock so hard."

`overheard.watch.5` — information (evening, in the yard: Vale, Stane)

1. Vale: "The bow on the wall, sir. Sedge's. The men ask why we keep it."
2. Stane: "Because he was the best shot in the valley and I took it off him. I should remember that I did."

`overheard.watch.6` — story (night, in the hall: Stane, alone)

1. Stane, to the empty table: "Second watch never came in."

## The tax cart

Every few days, from the Watch-house to Wick and back. Gant leads; Rikke and Hew ride with it. Its strongbox holds the tenth, and the Hollins' candlesticks.

`overheard.cart.1` — a tell (on the road: Hew, Gant)

1. Hew: "The Marrin woman gave me bread last time. After I'd taken her flour."
2. Gant: "Eat it and be quiet."

`overheard.cart.2` — information (on the road: Gant, Rikke)

1. Gant: "We stop at the ford to water the horse. Every time. If I were robbing us, that's where I'd do it."
2. Rikke: "Good thing nobody's robbing us."

## The valley learns

`GAME_DESIGN.md` section 5. When the hero has leaned on one way of taking a place, the next place still held prepares for it, and the player hears why. Each exchange is overheard once at that place, early, by any two defenders there who are not its leader. `{A}` and `{B}` are their names.

`overheard.learns.helmets` — information (after many head shots)

1. {A}: "Helmets on. Even at supper. Captain's order."
2. {B}: "For one apprentice?"
3. {A}: "For one apprentice who doesn't miss."

`overheard.learns.night` — information (after attacks at night)

1. {A}: "Twice the torches and twice the watch, from dusk."
2. {B}: "We'll be seen for miles."
3. {A}: "That's the idea. It's the dark that one likes."

`overheard.learns.pairs` — information (after knockouts, or defenders picked off alone)

1. {A}: "Nobody walks alone. Not to the woodpile, not to the privy."
2. {B}: "And if I'd rather?"
3. {A}: "Then you'd rather wake up in Wick with a sore head. In twos."

`overheard.learns.bell` — information (after a bell was silenced or its runner stopped)

1. {A}: "Two on the bell now. One runs, one follows."
2. {B}: "And if the first one drops?"
3. {A}: "That's what the second one's for."

`overheard.learns.fire` — information (after fire was used)

1. {A}: "Grass is cut back to the fence. Buckets by every door."
2. {B}: "I joined up for the suppers. Nobody said burning."

A player who varies their approach hears none of these.

## If they stay

What each of those most likely to stay does in Wick. Hesper's line names the trade; these are their own first lines there.

| Who | Takes up | Says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dob Finch | Milling, for the Marrins | "Ansa Marrin looked at me a long time. Then she handed me a sack. I'll take that." |
| Peg Rudge | Striking, for Aldric | "He hasn't spoken to me. He hasn't told me to leave the fire alone, either." |
| Tobin Pike | Farming, and the company | "My mother cried. My father shook my hand. I don't know which was worse." |
| Ketch | Shepherding, for the Sedges | "Old Sedge counted the sheep, then me, then the sheep again." |
| Hew Pollard | Thatching | "I'm mending every roof I ever knocked under. For nothing. It seems fair." |
| Pim | Running errands for the whole village | "They send me for things and I come back and they say thank you. Every time." |
| Bran Oakes | Ploughing | "Nobody's shouted at me for sleeping in a week. I've been too tired to sleep." |
| Wyll Carrow | Fowling | "The fisher says I scare the fish. I say he scares the ducks. We're getting on." |
| Jory Flint | Fletching, for the Tolleys | "Ada Tolley says my fletching's a disgrace. She's showing me. She's right." |
| Odo Varn | Droving, with the Wains | "I know every road out of here. Never thought I'd be glad to take one and come back." |

Anyone else who stays takes up the trade in their table row and says: "I'd forgotten I knew how."

## In place of Tobin

If Tobin Pike was killed, Ketch can be recruited in his place once won back, with the same trait as a companion and his own lines, which Viktor may draft in his voice: young, quick, and glad to be anywhere else.
