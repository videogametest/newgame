# Inside

What is in each building the player can enter. Use the layouts, objects, and words exactly as written. `GAME_DESIGN.md` section 4 (Inside) has the rules.

## Layouts

Six rooms, each one room on the ground floor, reused and dressed differently. Upper floors are never entered: a ladder or stair goes up into shadow.

| Layout | Size | Always has |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cottage | Small, one window | Hearth, bed, table, one chair, a shelf |
| Farmhouse | Medium, two windows | Hearth, long table with benches, two beds or more, a dresser |
| Workshop | Medium, wide door | A workbench, the trade's main tool, a small hearth, a cot |
| Shop | Medium, a counter facing the door | Counter, shelves behind it, a back table, a bed behind a curtain |
| Inn | Large | A bar, three tables, a big hearth, a stair up |
| Hall | Large, stone | One long table, benches, a hearth at the end, hooks on the walls |

## Rules for dressing a room

- **Every room says who lives there** in three to six objects. A stranger should guess the trade from the doorway.
- **A room is lived in.** A pot on the hearth, a coat on a hook, something half done on the table.
- **Returned things are on display.** A stolen thing given back appears in its owner's room, in the place named below. A sold one leaves that place visibly empty.
- **At night the hearth and one candle are lit,** and that is the light seen in the window from outside.
- **The people are in.** After dusk the household is inside, at the places named. By day the room is usually empty and can be looked round.
- Looking at an object gives its line, if it has one, as a small caption. Captions are in the voice of the screen: plain, no exclamation marks.

## Hesper's cottage

Cottage. Open from the start.

- **The lamp** in the window, always lit. Caption: "It has not been out in twenty years."
- **The storage chest** at the foot of the bed.
- **Two hooks by the door.** One holds her cloak. The other is empty. Caption on the empty one: "A second hook. Nothing has hung on it for a long time." After the finale the hero's old grey cloak hangs there.
- **A small bell with no clapper** on the mantel, until she gives it away.
- **One bowl, one cup, one chair.** When Bram arrives, a second stool appears and never leaves.
- Hesper sits in the chair facing the window, not the fire.

## Old Maren's

Cottage. Two doors from Hesper's. Dark until her window is lit; after that, open.

- **A chair at the window,** worn through at the arms.
- **The cupboard** with the second lamp (`stories.md`, story 5). After step 2 the lamp stands in the window; after "leave it", the helmet beside it.
- **Sixteen pebbles on the sill,** in a row. One is moved to the other end for each household home. Caption: "Somebody has been keeping count."

## The Marrins' (the Mill, ground floor)

Workshop. An enemy place first: defenders eat here at midday and dusk.

- **The millstones,** turning when the Mill is free and the wheel runs.
- **Meal bins** along the back wall. Behind them, the mill bill (`stories.md`, story 1).
- **A bread oven.** Cold while the Mill is held. Warm, with loaves, once the Marrins are home.
- **A nail by the door** at wedding-ring height. Returned: the ring hangs there on a thread. Caption: "She does not wear it. She likes to see it when she comes in."
- Held by Stane's men: sacks stamped with his mark, spears in the corner, a dice game on a barrel.

## The Aldrics' (the Forge)

Workshop, open-fronted. An enemy place first.

- **The anvil and the fire.** The fire is never out, whoever holds the place.
- **The hammer's place:** two pegs on the wall. Returned: the hammer hangs there when Bertram is not using it. Sold: the pegs stay bare.
- **The scrap heap,** with the flawed blade (`stories.md`, story 2).
- **The store:** a locked room at the back, the strongbox inside. Hob Tarn sleeps across its door. The coal hatch opens into it from behind the building.
- **Two cots.** After the striker's story ends with Peg staying, a third.

## The Sedges'

Farmhouse.

- **Shepherd's crooks** by the door, one short.
- **A dog's blanket** by the hearth. Bracken sleeps there unless he is travelling.
- **Two pegs over the mantel.** Returned: Hale's bow rests there until he gives it to Tamsin, and after that the pegs are empty and he has hung a sprig of yarrow on them.
- **Deer antlers** nobody mentions.

## The Pikes'

Farmhouse.

- **Three places laid at the table** from the day they return, whether or not Tobin is alive.
- **A bed-post notched for birthdays,** fifteen notches.
- If the tally was shown to Ned: the stick on the mantel, with a knife cut across each notch as it is worked off.

## The Wains'

Cottage. The one with a window facing the lake.

- **Almost nothing.** A bed, a chest, one chair, a jar of flowers. They came with a cart and no furniture.
- A second chair appears after `wains.settled`.

## The Kettles'

Shop. The general trader.

- **Shelves of everything:** pots, rope, lanterns, three identical kettles.
- **A handcart** inside the door, never unpacked.

## The Brands'

Cottage, at the forest edge of the village.

- **Charcoal sacks** stacked to the roof.
- **Three axes,** in sizes.
- Soot on everything. Caption on the table: "Somebody has wiped one clean place, for eating."

## Oona's

Cottage.

- **Herbs hanging from every beam.**
- **A table of jars,** each with a label nobody else can read.
- **A cot for patients.** A wounded companion who is not travelling lies here.

## The Hollins' (the inn)

Inn. Closed and roofless until the inn is rebuilt; Bess lives in the one dry back room till then, and that room is open.

- **The bar.** Returned: the brass candlesticks stand on it, lit at night. Before the inn is rebuilt they stand on an upturned crate in the back room.
- **Three tables.** After dusk, whoever is home in Wick and has no reason to be elsewhere is here.
- **A slate** with a tally of what the hero owes: always nothing. Caption: "First round's free."

## The Tolleys'

Workshop.

- **Bow staves** in every stage, clamped along one wall.
- **The leather roll.** Returned: open on the bench, every tool in its loop.
- **A fletching bench** by the window with better light. That one is Ada's.

## The Ashbys'

Cottage.

- **Candles drying on racks,** hundreds.
- **A bee skep** indoors, empty, kept for luck.

## The Loomises'

Workshop.

- **The loom,** filling half the room. Still, with a half-woven cloth, until the shuttle is returned. Working after.
- **A small stool** beside the big one.
- **Cloaks on a rail:** grey, green, and blue.

## The Dunns'

Farmhouse.

- **Six beds** in a room built for three.
- **Chalk drawings** low on one wall: a house, a wolf, a figure with a bow.
- Returned: the carved horse is never in the same place twice.

## The Rudds'

Cottage, beside the bell post.

- **A coil of bell rope** on a hook. Gone from the hook once the bell is rehung.
- **A candle clock** marked for dusk.

## The Watch-house hall

Hall. An enemy place. Entered during or after the fight for it.

- **The supper table,** set for twelve, as it was left: bowls, cups, a loaf gone to stone, a jug. Dusted. `fragment.suppertable`, the duty slate, hangs by the door.
- **Hale Sedge's bow** on the wall, until taken.
- **One chair at the head, pushed back.** Stane's cloak lies over it after he leaves.
- Nothing here changes after the Watch-house is freed. Caption on the table, then: "Nobody has cleared it. Nobody will say why."

## The Fairlow house

Farmhouse. Boarded until the finale. Then the hero's.

- **The window,** with the lamp Hesper lights.
- **Racks, shelves, and a mantel,** empty at first, where weapons, unique items, and keepsakes are placed.
- **A cradle** in the corner, small enough for a bread basket. Caption: "It has been dusted."
- **Wrens under the eaves,** heard from inside.
