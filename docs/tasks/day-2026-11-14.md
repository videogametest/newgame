## Decisions taken

- [2026-11-14] Valley planning uses 0.5 m cells. At 1 m the stepping stones do not join (11 of 29 stone cells reachable from the west bank); at 0.5 m all 105 do. Measured: a 70 m local route takes about 260 ms mean and 461 ms worst at 1 m, about 1 s at 0.5 m. So fighters replan on a timer, not every frame.
- [2026-11-14] The time budget is 1 s for a 70 m local route at 1 m cells. This number is my choice; the design sets no pathfinding budget. Corner-to-corner routes across the whole valley are not a requirement: the rim and gorge split it, and the search returns null.
