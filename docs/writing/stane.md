# Captain Edric Stane

Use these lines exactly as written.

## How he speaks

- Formal and tired. A man who has given orders for twenty years and is no longer sure anyone should have obeyed them.
- Certain, until the bell. After it, plain.
- He does not sneer and he does not gloat. He believes he kept the valley alive, and he is partly right.
- He never uses a pronoun for Wren.

## Before the fight

The hero does not meet him until the Watch-house. Before that he is present only through what he has written.

`stane.notice.mill` (nailed to the Mill's door after the turn)

> This mill is under the protection of the Watch. It was, and it is again.
> E. Stane, Captain.

`stane.notice.tax` (posted in Wick while the Watch-house is held)

> The tenth is due at the new moon. The Watch has kept the roads for twenty years and asks no more than it needs.
> E. Stane, Captain.

## The fight

Lines above his head. In each group, one of, never the same twice running.

### When the hero enters the yard

`stane.fight.open`

1. "So you're what Hesper's been keeping the lamp for."
2. "You've taken my mill and my forge. You'll find this harder."

### Phase 1: commanding his guard

`stane.fight.command` (one of)

- "Shields up. Hold the line."
- "Left. Take the big one."
- "Don't chase. Make them come to you."
- "Archer. The one in the hood."
- "Close up. Close up."

`stane.fight.guard.falls` (one of, when one of his guard is beaten or breaks)

- "Stand fast."
- "Hold. I said hold."

### Phase 2: alone

`stane.fight.alone.start`

1. "Just us, then."

`stane.fight.alone` (one of)

- "I held this valley when nobody else would."
- "Twenty winters. Not one of them starved."
- "You think a lamp feeds anyone?"
- "Hesper sent you. She would. She never did her own asking."
- "I was guarding this gate before you could walk."

`stane.fight.alone.hit` (one of, when the hero lands a critical strike)

- "Good. She's taught you something."
- "Again, then."

### Phase 3: the bell

`stane.fight.bell.ring`

1. "To me! To the Watch-house!"

After a silence, one of the following, by what the player did.

`stane.fight.bell.empty` (nobody won back)

1. "Nobody."
2. "No. There wouldn't be."

`stane.fight.bell.answered` (bandits won back stand at the gate; `{names}` lists up to three of them)

1. "{names}."
2. "You too."
3. "So that's how it is."

### The yield

`stane.fight.yield`

1. "Enough. I yield."
2. "I know when a post is lost. I've had twenty years to learn it."

### If the hero falls

`stane.fight.herofalls` (one of)

- "Go home, apprentice. Tell Hesper I said good evening."
- "Get up. Or don't. I'll be here."

## After the fight

The reunion with Hesper is written in full in `hesper.md` under `finale.reunion`.

### What he saw

Spoken to the hero after the reunion, as a conversation.

`stane.account`

1. "The beacon was lit. You've been up the hill. You know you can see it from there."
2. "Every soldier in that yard had sworn to march when it burned. They marched."
3. "I stood in the gate and watched them go. Somebody had to stay."
4. "Then I went after the ones who'd slipped the column. The ones who'd sooner stay. It took a week."
5. "When I came back with them, Hesper looked at me like a thief. I let her. It was easier than explaining."
6. "I told myself it was duty. Maybe I was only afraid of what was at the other end of the road."
7. "I broke my oath to keep these people fed. I'd do that again. I'd do the rest differently."

### Show

`stane.show`

- Wren's bow: "Wren's. Wren was the last one through the gate. Looked back at the Fairlow house the whole way to the bend. Then went."
- His own order from the cave: "A tenth. Yes. It was meant to be fair. It stopped feeling fair a long time ago."
- The duty roster: "My hand. I wrote the watches out every evening. That was the last one."
- The letter from the soldiers' road: "Dav Marrin. He asked me to see it got to Ansa. I never did. Give it to her."
- One of his banners: "Take it down. I should have, years ago."
- Anything else: "I've nothing to say about that."

### Leaving

He takes off the captain's cloak.

`stane.leave`

1. "I never went to see what the beacon was for."
2. "I think it's time I did."
3. "Keep the valley better than I did. It won't be hard."

To Hesper, last:

4. "Keep the lamp."
