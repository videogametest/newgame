# Bram

The friend. Use these lines exactly as written.

## How he speaks

- Cheerful, plain, and a little too loud.
- He says what the hero would say if the hero spoke, and then some things the hero would not.
- His jokes are at his own expense or at the situation's. Never cruel, never at the villagers'.
- Under the jokes he is steady. When things are bad he stops joking and says something short.
- He talks to the hero as someone he has known all his life, and takes the hero's silence as an answer.
- He never uses a pronoun for Wren.

## Arrival

Walking into the valley at dusk.

`bram.arrival` (in order, spaced along the walk)

1. "That's it? That's the valley? You talked about it for ten years. It's very green."
2. "One window. Well. That's one more than I expected."
3. "If there's soup at the end of this I'll forgive you the walk."

After meeting Hesper:

`bram.arrival.hesper`

1. "She likes me. I can tell."

## In the field

One of, in the right situation, never the same twice running.

`bram.field.crouched` (whispered, while the hero is crouched and watching)

- "I'm crouching. I want it noted."
- "How long do we watch? I've counted the geese twice."
- "My knees are making a noise. Can they hear my knees?"
- "You look. I'll be quiet. Watch me be quiet."

`bram.field.hold` (given the hold order)

- "Staying. Shout if you die."
- "Right here. Not moving. A rock."

`bram.field.follow` (given the follow order)

- "With you."
- "About time."

`bram.field.alive.on` (the take-them-alive order turned on)

- "Flat of the blade. Got it. Gently. Ish."
- "Nobody dies. Understood. I'll tell my shield."

`bram.field.alive.off`

- "Properly, then."

`bram.field.secret` (the hero finds a place off the path)

- "How do you see these things? I'd have walked straight past."
- "Now that's worth the detour."

`bram.field.wolves` (night, wolves heard)

- "Wolves. Far off. Stay near the light. I'm not carrying you."

## In a fight

One of, in the right situation, never the same twice running.

`bram.fight.start`

- "Here we go."
- "Behind me. Or not. You never are."

`bram.fight.guardbreak` (he has broken a shield-bearer's guard)

- "He's open. Now!"
- "Shield's down. Take it!"

`bram.fight.attention` (several enemies on him)

- "Take your time. I'm only being hit."
- "All of them? Fine. All of them."

`bram.fight.heroshot` (the hero lands a weak-point hit near him)

- "That went past my ear."
- "I felt that one go by."

`bram.fight.wounded` (he falls)

- "I'm fine. I'm sitting down, but I'm fine."

`bram.fight.won`

- "Is that all of them? Tell me that's all of them."
- "I'll be honest, I enjoyed that."

`bram.fight.won.mercy` (a place taken with most defenders spared)

- "You know what, I like that you didn't."

`bram.fight.won.killing` (a place taken with nobody spared)

- "Did you have to? All right. All right."

## The lanterns

`bram.lantern.first`

1. "Look at that. I'll never say it to her face, but she's right about the lamp."

`bram.lantern.later` (one of)

- "Another one. You can see three from here now."
- "It's starting to look like somewhere."

## The turn

The morning after the Mill is retaken. He does not joke.

`bram.turn`

1. "We'll get it back. We got it once."
2. "Say something. No? Fine. I'll say it. That was a rotten night."

## Campfires

Spoken to the hero while resting. One per rest, in order, each needing the stage of bond shown. The hero does not answer; leave a pause where he waits for one.

`bram.camp.1` (bond 1)

1. "You know why I came? Somebody had to carry the food. You'd have starved by the second hill."

`bram.camp.2` (bond 1)

1. "My mum said, 'Bram, don't follow that one into trouble.'"
2. "I said, 'It's a valley, Mum. How much trouble is in a valley.'"

`bram.camp.3` (bond 1)

1. "I've been thinking. You sneak, I get hit. When do we swap?"
2. "We don't swap. No. I didn't think so."

`bram.camp.4` (bond 2, after the first trial is passed)

1. "She told you 'again' eleven times today. I counted. I think that's her being fond of you."

`bram.camp.5` (bond 2)

1. "Do you ever think about Wren? Sorry. Fire makes me ask things."
2. "You don't have to answer. You won't. But you don't have to."

`bram.camp.6` (bond 2, after the turn)

1. "I was scared, the night they took the Mill back. I'm telling you because you won't tell anyone."

`bram.camp.7` (bond 3)

1. "When this is done I'm sleeping in a bed for a week."
2. "Then I suppose we go and see what's past that castle. Don't look at me like that. I know you."

`bram.camp.8` (bond 3)

1. "I'd do it again, you know. The walk. All of it."
2. "Don't make it strange. Pass the bread."

## The finale

`bram.finale.before` (as Hesper leads the hero to the Fairlow house)

1. "Go on. She's waiting. I'll be at the festival, eating everything."

`bram.finale.after` (the next morning)

1. "Seventeen. I counted too." (Only if all sixteen households are home. Otherwise use the other line of `bram.finale.count` in `households.md`.)
