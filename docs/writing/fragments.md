# Fragments

The words found in the ten places off the path, and a few others. Use them exactly as written.

## Rules for every fragment

- Three sentences at most.
- Each tells the player one thing and no more. Together they let the player work out what `GAME_DESIGN.md` section 9 says the first valley reveals: there was a beacon, it was lit, the guard marched, Hesper was beyond the ridge, Stane stayed, and Wren went.
- None of them says, or hints, why the beacon was lit or what became of the soldiers.
- No pronoun is ever used for Wren.
- Each can be shown to Hesper, and some to Stane, the fisher, or Old Maren. Their answers are in `hesper.md` and `stane.md`.
- Each is added to the Notes tab when found, under its title.

## The patrol hut beyond the ridge

A one-room hut. From its door the ridge fills the sky to the west; the castle cannot be seen. On the table, a logbook open at its last entry.

`fragment.patrolhut` — *A patrol log*

> Fourth night out. All quiet past the ridge. Returning at first light.
> H.

## The signal hill

The highest point in the valley. A cold iron brazier on a stone base. From here the castle is plain on the horizon, and on its tallest tower, a second brazier, dark.

`fragment.signalhill` — *The relay stone*

Cut into the base:

> RELAY OF TALLOWMERE
> When the castle burns, answer, and march.

## The soldiers' road

An overgrown track leading out of the valley toward the castle. A helmet on a fencepost. A cart, half loaded, its shafts on the ground. A single boot. Under the cart's seat, a folded paper.

`fragment.soldiersroad.helmet` — *A guard's helmet*

No words. It can be carried and shown.

`fragment.soldiersroad.letter` — *An unsent letter*

> Ansa. No time. The hill's lit and we're called.
> Keep the oven warm. I'll be home before the bread's cold.
> Dav

## The supper table

Inside the Watch-house. A long table laid for a meal: bowls, spoons, a loaf gone to stone. Stane has never let anyone clear it. On the wall, a slate.

`fragment.suppertable` — *The duty slate*

> E. Stane: the gate.
> Hesper: patrol, east ridge, four nights.
> W. Fairlow: second watch.

## The family markers

Stones along the road into Wick, one for each family that left, each cut with a name. Most have only the name. A lantern appears on a stone when its family comes home.

`fragment.markers.fairlow` — *The Fairlow stone*

> FAIRLOW

Below the name, scratched in by a different hand, much later:

> Come home.

`fragment.markers.others`

Each of the other fifteen stones carries only the household's name, from data.

## Wren's practice ground

A clearing in the forest. Three archery targets, rotted and leaning. A marked stone to shoot from. On the oak behind the stone, two carvings, one above the other.

`fragment.practiceground` — *Carved in the oak*

The lower, older, in neat letters:

> H. says: again.

The upper, deeper, in a hurry:

> W. F. hit it. Three of three. H. said nothing.

`fragment.practiceground.bow` — *Wren's bow*

Hidden in the hollow of the oak, wrapped in oilcloth. No words. On the grip, the wear of one hand.

## The cave behind the waterfall

A bandit stash: sacks, crates, a strongbox. Pinned to a crate, an order.

`fragment.cave` — *A standing order*

> Take the tenth, not the half. Nobody in Wick goes hungry on our account.
> Any man who takes more answers to me.
> E. Stane, Captain

## The wild orchard

Fruit trees gone wild, and a row of old hives in the long grass. A board nailed to the first tree, the paint almost gone.

`fragment.orchard` — *A painted board*

> SEDGE'S ORCHARD
> Pick what you need. Leave the hives alone. They know who you are.

## The firefly grove

A spring in a hollow, hidden by alder. A flat stone beside the water, worn smooth in two places, side by side.

There are no words here. Nothing is collected and nothing is added to Notes.

## The fisher on the lake

An old fisher in a flat boat, who never left the valley and never paid anyone.

`fisher.greeting` (one of)

- "I paid the Crown nothing and I pay Stane nothing. The fish don't ask who's king."
- "You're Hesper's new one. You'll want to sit still. The last one couldn't."

`fragment.fisher` — *What the fisher saw* (told once the hero has bought fish twice, or shown the helmet or the letter)

1. "I saw them go, that night. The whole column on the road, torches, nobody talking."
2. "One of them looked back the whole way to the bend."
3. "Here's what I never told Hesper. The hill was lit a good hour before they marched. They argued first."

`fisher.teach` (when the fisher teaches the perk)

1. "You watch the float, not the fish. Same with an arrow. Watch where it's going, not where it is."

## Old Maren

She never left. She remembers everyone.

`maren.greeting` (one of)

- "Fairlow's child. You were so small you fitted in a bread basket. I know, I carried it."
- "Sit down. I'll tell you who lived where. Somebody ought to know."

`maren.show`

- A stolen thing belonging to a household: "Oh, that's {name}'s. They wept when it went. Take it to them."
- The letter from the soldiers' road: "Dav always was late for everything. He'd be sorry to be late for this."
- The Fairlow stone's second line: "That's Hesper's hand. Don't tell her I told you."
- Wren's bow: "Wren used to shoot the apples off my tree. I never minded. I said I minded."
- Anything else: "I don't know it. But I'm glad somebody's finding things."

`fragment.maren` — *What Maren remembers* (told once four windows are lit)

1. "The morning after, the Watch-house gate was standing open and the supper was still on the table."
2. "Hesper came down off the ridge at noon and stood in that yard a long time."
3. "Then she went home and lit a lamp. In the middle of the day."
